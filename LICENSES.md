The project encourages:
- community contribution
- tooling improvements
- interoperability

while protecting:
- operational intelligence
- telemetry-derived value
- curated hauling knowledge

## Contributor Rule
By contributing, contributors agree that:
- code contributions fall under MIT unless otherwise specified
- data/docs/prompts contributions fall under CC BY-NC 4.0
- telemetry submissions may become part of the public dataset
