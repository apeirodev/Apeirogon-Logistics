# DERIVATION RULES

Operational penalties in Phase 3 are derived heuristics and not authoritative gameplay facts unless directly sourced later.

Core principles:
- Surface destinations are operationally more expensive than orbital destinations.
- Cities incur atmosphere, spline, tram, congestion, and hangar traversal penalties.
- Outposts carry elevated dead-leg risk due to likely lack of continuation hauling opportunities.
- Orbital stations are considered high-efficiency route anchors.
- QT time is not stored here and remains runtime-derived.

Penalty scale:
0 = negligible
10 = severe operational burden

These values are intended for comparative routing optimization, not exact simulation.
