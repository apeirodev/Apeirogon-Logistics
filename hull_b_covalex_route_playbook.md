# Hull-B Covalex Route Playbook

## Core Principle
Optimize for reputation progression and operational efficiency, not maximum payout per mission.

## Screenshot Analysis Workflow
1. Identify all pickup locations.
2. Group missions sharing the same pickup point.
3. Identify overlapping delivery destinations.
4. Reject isolated dead-leg routes unless payout or reputation gain justifies them.
5. Prioritize orbital-loop continuity where possible.

## Same-Pickup Stacking
High-value stacks:
- identical pickup
- overlapping orbital destinations
- overlapping moon distributions
- minimal atmosphere transitions

## Route Poison Indicators
Reject or deprioritize:
- isolated atmosphere-heavy deliveries
- single-stop dead-leg missions
- low-density regional outliers
- excessive unloading fragmentation

## Hull-B Operational Constraints
Distinguish:
- nominal cargo capacity
- operational comfort capacity
- unloading complexity
- external cargo handling exposure
- atmosphere traversal burden

## OCR Handling
If screenshots are unclear:
- preserve unresolved values
- do not invent station names
- do not infer quantities
- flag unreadable entries explicitly

## Route Sequencing
Recommended order:
1. pickup consolidation
2. orbital drops
3. moon drops
4. atmosphere-heavy final deliveries
5. recovery positioning for next chain
